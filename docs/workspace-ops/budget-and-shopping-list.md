# Budget & Shopping List

Concrete things to buy (with real products and suppliers) at three budget levels, plus DIY
options that use the 3D printers you already have. Prices are ballpark **CAD** and change — treat
them as relative sizing, not quotes.

!!! tip "Sequence your spend"
    You don't need everything before the blitz. **Must-haves for blitz day:** bins/totes, a label
    printer or Avery labels, colored tape, red tags, trash/recycling, gloves. **Everything else**
    (shadow boards, locks, LiPo storage) can follow in the week after. Buy storage *after* Phase 3
    when you know your zones — measuring twice saves a return trip.

## 1. Three budget tiers

## 2. Master shopping checklist (by function)

!!! abstract "Storage & bins"
    - [ ] Back-room shelving (wire or rivet) — size to your wall after Phase 3
    - [ ] Divided small-parts boxes for HW (screws by length), electronics
    - [ ] Team totes (one per team, lidded, in team colors if available)
    - [ ] Line-side kanban bins (2× per high-churn consumable)
    - [ ] Kit totes/bags for issuing BOMs
    - [ ] Red-tag holding totes

!!! abstract "Labeling & color"
    - [ ] Label printer (Brother P-touch / Dymo) **or** Avery sheet labels
    - [ ] Extra label tape — including **colored** tape for category stripes
    - [ ] Colored electrical/gaffer tape — one distinct color per team
    - [ ] Colored dot stickers (quick team/category marking)
    - [ ] Laminating pouches for SOPs & the zone map
    - [ ] QR codes (generated by your app, or via a Sheets add-on)

!!! abstract "Tools & organization"
    - [ ] Pegboard + hooks, **or** French-cleat panels, per station
    - [ ] **Kaizen/shadow foam** for drawer tool silhouettes
    - [ ] Paint pen / marker for shadow outlines
    - [ ] A dedicated set of VEX tools per team (5/64" & 3/32" hex, nut drivers) in team colors

!!! abstract "Security & access"
    - [ ] Lockset for back-room door **or** hasp + padlock on a cabinet
    - [ ] Keyed/combo locks for A-item cabinet
    - [ ] Optional team-bin locks
    - [ ] Check-out clipboard (paper) or tablet (digital)

!!! danger "Safety (don't skip — this is a minors' space)"
    - [ ] LiPo/Li-ion **fire-safe storage** (bag → box → steel cabinet as budget allows)
    - [ ] Supervised charging station with **per-bay timers**
    - [ ] Fire extinguisher rated for the space (ABC; consider Class-D for lithium)
    - [ ] PPE station: safety glasses at every cut/drill/print/solder spot
    - [ ] First-aid kit, clearly located
    - [ ] SDS binder for chemicals (CA glue, Loctite, solder flux, lubricants)
    - [ ] Lock-out plan for band saw / bar cutter / anything dangerous when unsupervised

## 3. Where to buy

Your repo's [Resources page](../resources/index.md) already lists robotics vendors. For the
*storage & organization* side:

| Need | Where |
|---|---|
| VEX parts & official part numbers | [VEX Robotics](https://www.vexrobotics.com/), [RoboSource](https://www.robosource.net/) |
| Fasteners, raw stock, bearings | [McMaster-Carr](https://www.mcmaster.com/) (fast, well-cataloged part numbers) |
| Bins, shelving, small-parts cabinets | Hardware stores (Home Depot/Lowe's/Canadian Tire), Uline, Akro-Mils, Amazon |
| Label printers & tape | Brother / Dymo (office & online retailers) |
| Kaizen/shadow foam | Automotive & tool retailers, Amazon |
| LiPo-safe storage & extinguishers | Hobby/RC shops, safety suppliers |

!!! tip "Reuse VEX's part numbers — don't reinvent them"
    VEX already assigns a part number to nearly everything (e.g. `276-xxxx`). Put that in your
    **Vendor PN** field and reordering becomes copy-paste. Your *internal* part # is just the
    human-readable shelf label; the VEX PN is the source of truth for buying.

## 4. DIY: 3D-printed storage {#diy-3d-printed-storage}

You teach 3D printing and have printers — use them. This is free storage *and* a great student
print project.

### Gridfinity

**[Gridfinity](https://gridfinity.xyz/)** is an open, modular bin system (a 42 mm grid of
baseplates + snap-in bins) with a huge free library of printable bins. It's ideal for VEX:

- Print bins sized exactly for **8-32 screws by length**, spacers, collars, bearings, small gears.
- Baseplates tile a drawer or shelf; bins snap in and stay put (no sliding mess).
- Endlessly extendable — add bins as you add SKUs, print replacements when they break.
- Print in **team colors** for at-a-glance ownership.

### Other useful prints

- **Label holders / tag frames** that clip onto bins and totes.
- **Shadow-board tool holders** for oddly-shaped tools.
- **Cable/wire spool holders** for smart cables and BattleBots wire.
- **Filament & battery organizers** with slots for date/health tags.

!!! example "Industry example — point-of-use, made-to-fit storage"
    Lean shops build **custom point-of-use holders** so each item has an obvious, made-to-fit
    home (part of "Set-in-order"). Gridfinity is the maker-community version of exactly that —
    and printing your own bins turns a chore into a build project the students own.

## 5. A note on funding

Storage, shelving, a label printer, and LiPo-safe cabinets are **concrete, photographable,
safety-relevant** asks — the kind STEM grants and local sponsors say yes to. Use the **"before"
photos** from Phase 0 and a one-page version of this plan as the pitch: *"We're professionalizing
a shared workspace used by N student teams; here's the plan and the parts list."* That story funds
itself far more easily than "we need money for robots."
